## From "Usable" to "Delightful"

With the foundation in place, we entered the polishing stage. The goal was not new features, but making every function evolve from "usable" to "delightful."

## Key Updates

- **Bilingual UI** (v1.4.0): Chinese and English interfaces to accommodate global teams
- **Perfect Format Retention**: Translated Word/PPT documents retain bold, italic, underline, text color, and highlights — the document no longer loses its "soul"
- **Bilingual Output** (v1.5.0): Side-by-side Chinese-English display for easy original text reference
- **Glossary Right-Click Management**: Quickly add/remove professional terms for accurate translation

> Reducing repetitive work has always been our unwavering mission.
