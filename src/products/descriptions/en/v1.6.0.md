## Conquering the "Ultimate Form" of Documents

**v1.6.0** officially introduced PDF direct translation. Due to its fixed layout and complex structure, PDF has long been the toughest challenge in document processing. Through multiple iterations — improving layout analysis, recognizing text alignment, supporting merged table cells — File Translator finally gained the ability to handle this "ultimate form" of documents.
