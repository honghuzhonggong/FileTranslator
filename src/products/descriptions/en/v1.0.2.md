## Feedback from the Front Lines

After releasing v1.0.0, early users in the team immediately flagged a core pain point: **entering cell addresses one by one was too cumbersome**. Translating columns A through D required typing A, B, C, D individually.

## Two Rapid Iterations

**v1.0.1**: Clarified comma delimiter conventions, laying a solid foundation.

**v1.0.2**: Introduced colon `:` syntax for continuous ranges. Translating columns A through D now requires just typing `A:D`.

## Key Takeaway

> Users are the best product mentors. The value of agility lies in rapid response — listen, improve, repeat.
