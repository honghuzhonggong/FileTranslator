## From Excel to Word & PPT

Once Excel translation stabilized, we set our sights higher: could Word and PowerPoint also benefit from AI translation?

**v1.1.0** officially added Word and PPT translation support. This seemingly small step opened a brand-new frontier. Unlike structured Excel, Word and PPT are filled with complex formatting, text boxes, and diverse objects.

## Engine & Interface Upgrades

**Broader model ecosystem**: DeepSeek, GLM, and other mainstream models integrated sequentially.

**Performance leap**: Multi-threading technology boosted long document translation speed several times over.

**v1.2.6 New UI**: Not just a visual refresh — a complete reconstruction of interaction logic.

## Milestone

From a "single-function tool" to a "comprehensive translation assistant" — a lightweight yet all-in-one office translation platform began to take shape.
